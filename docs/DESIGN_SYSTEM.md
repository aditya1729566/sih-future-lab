# SIH Future Lab Design System

## Thesis

India's innovation network visualized as an engineered radial instrument: precise enough to feel credible, alive enough to feel optimistic.

## Tokens

- Graphite `#070A0B` — primary environment
- Carbon `#111719` — structural depth
- Mineral `#E8E6DD` — editorial light and text
- Steel `#829093` — metadata and secondary copy
- Saffron `#FF7417` — human energy and primary action
- Signal cyan `#54D8FF` — technology and system state

Typography uses Anek Latin Variable for large authored forms, DM Sans for readable copy and IBM Plex Mono for coordinates, sources, indices and system state.

## Composition

The 3D core is the single signature element. Sections alternate between spatial darkness and flat editorial fields. Grids are asymmetric and edge-aligned; repeated rounded cards and decorative glass effects are excluded.

## Motion

- Micro feedback: 180–350ms, `power3.out` character.
- Interface transitions: 350–700ms.
- Cinematic wipes: 700–1200ms, strong in/out curve.
- Scroll-linked motion follows physical distance rather than time.
- Reduced motion renders final states immediately and disables smooth scrolling.
